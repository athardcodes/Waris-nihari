Brand introduction

The taste of Lahore

Rich, slow-cooked, and deeply spiced — this is nihari the way Anarkali has always known it.

A quieter kind of luxury: warm steam, layered spice, slow braising, and a bowl meant to be shared with bread, garnish, and time.

This concept is intentionally restrained and premium, letting the food and the room carry the story.
